---
title: Reporting problems
description: How to report a bug or give feedback on XONOVA, and what to include.
---

XONOVA is in beta, and reports from people actually making music with it are what make it better.

## What to include

1. **System info** – open the **Global** page and click **Copy system info** in the About panel. It copies
   the XONOVA version, plugin format, host (DAW), operating system and audio settings. Paste it at the top
   of your report.
2. **What you did** – the steps that lead to the problem, as precisely as you can.
3. **What you expected** and **what happened instead**.
4. **The preset** – if it's about a sound, save it and attach the `.xnpreset` file (see
   [Saving presets](/xonova-docs/presets/saving/)).
5. **Screenshots or a short recording** help a lot, especially for interface or sound problems.

Feedback that isn't a bug is just as welcome: what you played, which DAW, and what felt off or what you
missed.

## Where to send it

- Reach out to **Monkey Business Soundsystem** via [mbss.be](https://mbss.be).
- Or contact the person who gave you your test build.
